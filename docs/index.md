# Where

Where records your location in the background and sends it to a server you
choose. It also lets you review recent routes, record trips, and optionally
export to your own iCloud Drive or Apple Health.

## Get help

For questions, bug reports, or feature requests, open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/SoPat712/Where/issues). Include your iOS version,
Where version, and what happened. Issues are public: leave out location history,
server credentials, and other private information.
